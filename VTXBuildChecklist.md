## FPV Camera & VTX Build Checklist

  

### Camera Installation

- [ ] Mount FPV camera securely on the airframe (ensure forward alignment)

- [ ] Verify lens orientation and tilt angle (typically 0–15° for trainers)

- [ ] Connect camera power leads to regulated voltage (usually 5V)

- [ ] Connect video signal wire to VTX input

- [ ] Check for proper strain relief on wires to avoid vibration damage

  

### VTX Installation

- [ ] Mount VTX in a location with airflow for cooling

- [ ] Connect VTX power to filtered supply (check voltage requirement: 7–26V typical)

- [ ] Connect video input from camera to VTX

- [ ] Attach antenna securely (MMCX/SMA connector tightened)

- [ ] Ensure antenna placement away from GPS and receiver antennas to reduce interference

  

### Wiring & EMI Mitigation

- [ ] Use twisted pairs or shielded wires for signal lines

- [ ] Add ferrite rings or LC filter if video noise observed

- [ ] Secure all wires with zip ties or heat shrink to prevent chafing

  

### Pre-Power Checks

- [ ] Verify correct voltage at camera and VTX before powering

- [ ] Confirm polarity on all connections

- [ ] Inspect for shorts or exposed solder joints

  

### Functional Test

- [ ] Power up system and verify live video feed on goggles/monitor

- [ ] Check VTX channel and band match receiver

- [ ] Set VTX power level to legal limit for bench test (usually PIT or 25mW)

- [ ] Confirm OSD overlay (if flight controller provides it)

  

### Final Prep for Flight

- [ ] Increase VTX power to desired level (within legal limits)

- [ ] Lock VTX channel to avoid accidental changes

- [ ] Confirm antenna orientation and secure mounting
